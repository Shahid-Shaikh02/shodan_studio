CalcAll

An all-in-one calculator for everyday math, dates, loans and health numbers.

CalcAll is an all-in-one calculator by Shodan Studio, built with Flutter and published as a web app. It covers basic arithmetic and scientific functions, age and date calculations, loan payments, interest and BMI, and lets you save your own formulas for calculations you use often.
